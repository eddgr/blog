title: Game Publishers with Launchers on Steam
slug: game-publishers-with-launchers-steam
date: 2026-04-28

Here are a list of game publishers on Steam that require an additional launcher:

- [EA](https://store.steampowered.com/publisher/EA)
- [Ubisoft](https://store.steampowered.com/publisher/ubisoft)
- [Rockstar Games](https://store.steampowered.com/publisher/rockstargames)
- [Blizzard](https://store.steampowered.com/publisher/blizzard)

You can go to the linked publisher page and hide all their games on your Steam store page by clicking on the Options icon and selecting `Ignore this creator`.
