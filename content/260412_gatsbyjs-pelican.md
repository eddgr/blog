title: GatsbyJS to Pelican
slug: gatsbyjs-pelican
date: 2026-04-12

I want to write for this site more frequently and I believe simplifying it is the way to do so.

My goal is to keep the design minimal and to reduce the friction in writing new posts.

Previously, I primarily used this site to showcase my earlier projects during my time at Flatiron School and was a way to show my frontend abilities.

However, I have not done frontend work in many years and now primarily focus on the backend. I also mostly my code in Python and SQL. So it didn't make sense to showcase projects that no longer reflect my current skills.

This leads into why I moved away from GatsbyJS, which was what I used previously. You can take a look at the [previous version of the site here](https://pensive-albattani-4c8772.netlify.app/).

I had a lot of fun learning to build with Gatsby and was part of the [Gatsby NYC Meetup](https://medium.com/@eddgr/contributing-to-my-first-open-source-project-gatsbynyc-18521c284297) but I just want to write and not worry about maintaining the framework.

Gatsby was very plugin dependent and many were no longer maintained. This was one of the reasons that made me consider moving to a different framework.

I thought by changing framework and reducing my dependencies, it would make me enjoy writing. After spending a week refactoring the whole site in Typescript for NextJS, I lost motivation. I had 90% of the work done but I was still focused on the design of the site, optimization of the pages for search engines, and other things not related to improving the writing experience.

That was a few years ago. I never implemented the changes.

Since then, every year I contemplate about refactoring the site to be more simple and writing focused.

This weekend I decided to redo this site using Pelican, a static site generator in Python. Most of my time was focused on tinkering with the Jinja templates and CSS to really simplify the site and to focus on how the content is presented.

The minimal design and the ability to write in markdown has lifted the friction I felt from the previous versions of the site.

I feel like I can finally focus on the writing. And here I am.
